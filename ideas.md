A fully English, single-page creative website presenting a cultural and artistic analysis of the 2024 Paris Olympics: Opening Ceremony, Closing Ceremony, Mascot, and key people/artworks/events.

## Idea A — "Parisian Noir Gazette"

**Design Movement:** Art Deco meets French Editorial Print (think 1920s Paris Match + Vogue covers)

**Core Principles:**
1. High-contrast ink-on-paper aesthetic — deep charcoal backgrounds with cream/ivory type
2. Asymmetric editorial grid — columns of varying widths, large pull-quotes breaking the flow
3. Ornamental dividers and ruled lines as structural elements, not decoration
4. Typography as illustration — oversized display text that functions as visual art

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: near-black charcoal (#1a1814) evoking aged newsprint at night
- Primary text: warm ivory (#f5f0e8) — not pure white, feels printed
- Accent: Olympic gold (#c9a84c) for headlines and highlights
- Secondary accent: deep tricolor red (#c8102e) for key moments

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Vertical scroll with editorial "spreads" — each section feels like a magazine page turn
- Staggered columns: text left, large image right, then flipped
- Pull-quotes set at 45° angles breaking out of columns
- Section dividers styled as newspaper mastheads

**Signature Elements:**
1. Ornate Art Deco border frames around key images
2. Vintage halftone dot texture overlaid on photographs
3. Section headers styled as newspaper front-page banners

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover reveals "ink bleed" effect on images (desaturate → sepia → color)
- Scroll-triggered text reveals that mimic a printing press stamping text
- Sticky navigation styled as a newspaper's section index

**Animation:**
- Page-load: text "types" in from left like a telegraph
- Section entrance: content slides in from below with a slight rotation (0.5°) then snaps level
- Image hover: subtle zoom + halftone overlay dissolves

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display Black (headlines, pull-quotes) — classical, editorial authority
- Body: Libre Baskerville Regular — readable, bookish, French academic feel
- Accent: Cormorant Garamond Italic — for captions and subheadings

## Idea B — "Luminous River" ← SELECTED

**Design Movement:** Contemporary French Minimalism meets Olympic Monumentalism — inspired by the Seine at dusk, Haussmann architecture, and the ceremony's own visual language

**Core Principles:**
1. Deep midnight blue as the dominant tone — the Seine at night, the sky over Paris
2. Gold as the singular accent — the Olympic flame, the Eiffel Tower's lights, the cauldron's glow
3. Generous vertical rhythm — sections breathe like acts in a theatrical performance
4. Imagery-first storytelling — full-bleed visuals anchor each section, text overlays with restraint

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: deep navy-to-midnight gradient (#0a0e1a → #12183a) — the Paris night sky
- Primary text: pure white for legibility against dark backgrounds
- Accent: luminous gold (#f0c040) — the Olympic flame, the cauldron, Parisian grandeur
- Tricolor accents: French blue (#002395), white, red (#ED2939) used sparingly for flags/moments
- Section dividers: thin gold horizontal rules

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-viewport hero with animated particle field (stars/sparks over the Seine)
- Alternating left/right content blocks with large images — not centered grids
- "Act" sections styled like theater playbills — large Roman numerals + act titles
- Horizontal scroll carousel for the 12 Opening Ceremony acts
- Timeline-style layout for the Closing Ceremony sequence

**Signature Elements:**
1. Thin gold rule lines as section separators, echoing the Olympic rings' geometry
2. Large Roman numerals (I–XII) as decorative act markers
3. Subtle ripple/water animation in the hero background — the Seine

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Scroll-triggered fade-and-rise for all content blocks
- Navigation dots on the right side (like a theater program's act list)
- Hover on act cards: gold border illuminates, image brightens

**Animation:**
- Hero: slow-moving particle field (gold sparks) over a dark Paris skyline
- Section entrance: elements rise 30px and fade in over 0.6s with staggered children
- Act cards: scale 1.02 on hover with gold glow shadow
- Cauldron section: pulsing warm glow animation

**Typography System:**
- Display: Cormorant Garamond (700 weight) — French elegance, classical authority
- Body: DM Sans (400/500) — clean, modern, highly readable on dark backgrounds
- Accent labels: DM Sans 600 uppercase with wide letter-spacing

## Idea C — "Mosaic Vivant"

**Design Movement:** Post-modern French Constructivism — inspired by the ceremony's own visual collage of eras

**Core Principles:**
1. Fragmented mosaic layout — content blocks arranged like ceramic tiles, irregular sizes
2. Bold color blocking — each section has its own dominant hue from the French tricolor palette
3. Collage aesthetic — overlapping text, images, and graphic elements
4. Kinetic energy — nothing is static, everything has a slight tilt or offset

**Color Philosophy:**
- Section 1 (Hero): Deep blue (#002395) — French Republic
- Section 2 (Opening): Warm cream (#faf5e4) with red accents
- Section 3 (Acts): Alternating blue/red/white blocks
- Section 4 (Closing): Near-black with gold
- Section 5 (Mascot): Bright red (#ED2939) — the Phryge itself

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Masonry-style card grid for the 12 acts
- Overlapping text boxes at slight rotations (±3°)
- Full-bleed color sections with large isolated typography
- "Torn paper" edge dividers between sections

**Signature Elements:**
1. Diagonal stripe patterns (inspired by the French flag's geometry)
2. Oversized isolated letters as background texture
3. Newspaper-clipping style image treatments

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Cards "assemble" on scroll — tiles fly in from random directions
- Hover tilts cards slightly (3D perspective transform)
- Color sections transition with a "paint sweep" wipe effect

**Animation:**
- Entrance: mosaic tiles assemble from scattered positions
- Hover: 3D card tilt (perspective: 1000px, rotateX/Y ±5°)
- Section transitions: diagonal color wipe

**Typography System:**
- Display: Space Grotesk Bold — geometric, modern, slightly quirky
- Body: Source Serif 4 — readable, editorial
- Accent: Space Grotesk Mono for labels and captions
